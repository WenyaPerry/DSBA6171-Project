INTERPRETATION RULES

1. "Minimum Credit Tier" represents the minimum risk tier required for the product.

2. Tier A represents the lowest modeled credit risk and Tier D represents the highest modeled credit risk.

3. "Minimum Income" represents the minimum annual income required before the application can qualify for the product.

4. "Maximum Loan Amount" represents the maximum requested amount permitted for the product.

5. "Maximum DTI" represents the maximum debt-to-income ratio permitted for standard eligibility.

6. "Review Level" identifies the expected level of underwriting review.

7. "Standard" indicates that the application may proceed through standard underwriting when all requirements are satisfied.

8. "Manual" indicates that the application requires human review.

9. "Enhanced" indicates additional documentation or risk review may be required.

10. Regional restrictions must be evaluated before approval.

PRODUCT REVIEW EXAMPLES

Example 1:
An applicant with Tier B credit, $52,000 annual income, and a $45,000 requested Personal Loan meets the listed minimum credit tier, minimum income, and maximum loan amount for PL-100. The application may proceed to standard underwriting unless another rule triggers manual review.

Example 2:
An applicant requesting $60,000 under PL-100 exceeds the product's $50,000 maximum loan amount. The application does not meet the listed product eligibility requirement.

Example 3:
An applicant with Tier C credit requesting a $60,000 Auto Loan may satisfy the credit-tier requirement and maximum amount requirement for PL-200, subject to the remaining eligibility requirements.

Example 4:
An applicant requesting a Small Business loan outside North Carolina does not satisfy the listed regional requirement for PL-500.
